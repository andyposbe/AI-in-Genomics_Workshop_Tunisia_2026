The Facilitator Programme

Ahead of the workshop, the cohort worked through a series of online sessions with the trainers: an introduction to the programme, AI in genomics, predicting protein structures, interpreting models, comparing models, AI-enabled design, and applications and opportunities. The aim is that facilitators can run these sessions themselves afterwards.
