## About the workshop

Artificial intelligence is changing how we translate genomic data into biological discovery. This hands-on workshop moves beyond theory to provide practical approaches for researchers in the post-genomic era.

Across two days, participants navigate a complete structural workflow: predicting protein architectures, interpreting molecular interactions and comparing structures across datasets, before bridging into protein design. An underlying thread will be that AI tools are a means to an end, not the end in and of themselves. We aim to build your confidence and skills to turn raw sequences into real-world structural insight.

## Before you arrive

Please come to the workshop with the software already installed.

1. Install UCSF ChimeraX from <https://www.cgl.ucsf.edu/chimerax/download.html>, making sure to select the correct version for your computer's operating system (see the list under *Other releases*).
2. Open ChimeraX once to confirm that it launches.
3. Create an AlphaFold Server account at <https://alphafoldserver.com/>.

Full instructions are on the [Before you arrive](setup.md) page.

If you run into problems installing or opening ChimeraX, email <andres.posbeyikian@tsl.ac.uk> before the workshop. Do not worry if it is not resolved by then, as we can troubleshoot together during the session, but it would help if as many of you as possible could have ChimeraX ready beforehand.

You will need to bring your own laptop. Computers are not provided at either venue.

## The practical sessions

- **[Practical 1: Predict](practicals/01-predict.md)** · Submit your sequence data to predict models, interpret and compare scores across complexes. Day 1, 11:20.
- **[Practical 2: Interpret](practicals/02-interpret.md)** · Introduction to molecular visualisation to interpret protein models and design key experiments. Day 1, 15:15.
- **[Practical 3: Compare](practicals/03-compare.md)** · Align structures to reveal hidden relationships beyond sequence similarity. Day 2, 09:30.
- **[Practical 4: Design](practicals/04-design.md)** · Design de novo structures and binders against a target to create novel solutions. Day 2, 13:30.

The step-by-step guide for each session is on its own page above, under **Practicals** in the menu. We add to these pages as the sessions run, so refresh the page if you are following along on a laptop.

Pre-computed results for every step are in the [data](https://github.com/andyposbe/AI-in-Genomics_Workshop_Tunisia_2026/tree/main/data) folder, so you can rejoin a session at any point.

## After the workshop

We keep this repository up to date once the workshop is over.

- Slide decks from the talks and session introductions go into [`slides/`](https://github.com/andyposbe/AI-in-Genomics_Workshop_Tunisia_2026/tree/main/slides), within two weeks of each workshop.
- Printable worksheets for each practical go into [`worksheets/`](https://github.com/andyposbe/AI-in-Genomics_Workshop_Tunisia_2026/tree/main/worksheets).
- The practical pages stay online and are corrected and extended after each run, so the version you see here is the current one.
