## How the sessions run

Work in pairs. There is more material on each page than fits the slot, which is deliberate. The **core path** is what we get through together; everything under *Going further* is yours to take home.

Checkpoints mark the places where everyone needs to be in the same state before we move on. If you are behind at a checkpoint, take the pre-computed files from `data/` and carry on. Falling behind on a download is not the same as falling behind on the science.

Every session ends with an open slot for **your own protein**. Bring a sequence you actually care about.

## If something breaks

1. Check the troubleshooting section at the foot of the page.
2. Put a sticky note on your laptop lid and a facilitator will come to you.
3. Use the pre-computed results in `data/` and keep moving.
