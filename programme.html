Useful information

Registration is required but free. Registration has now closed.

We are anticipating ~75 participants including facilitators. Most participants are from major institutes across Tunisia, and we are also welcoming international participants.

You will need to bring your own laptop.

Lunch and refreshments will be provided. Tell us about dietary requirements as early as you can so we can do our best to accommodate them.

You will need to arrange your own transport to and from the venue.

Please contact getgenome@tsl.ac.uk if you have any questions.
