FAQ

Attending

Is registration free? Registration is required but free, and it has now closed. Lunch and refreshments are provided.

Do I need to bring a laptop? Yes. Computers are not provided at either venue.

Do you cover travel? No. Please arrange your own transport to and from the venue.

I have a dietary requirement. Email getgenome@tsl.ac.uk as early as you can.

Can I attend both workshops? They run the same programme, so there is no benefit to attending twice. Places are limited; take the one nearer to you.

What time should I arrive? Registration opens 08:30 on Day 1, and 09:00 on Day 2. Arrive in plenty of time. We begin strictly on time.

Preparation

How much programming do I need? Very little. You need to be able to open a terminal and run a command that is written down for you. No prior Python is assumed.

Do I need to know structural biology? No. Day 1 morning covers the theory from the start.

How many people will be there? Around 75 including facilitators, mostly from institutes across Tunisia, plus international participants.

Should I bring my own data? Yes, if you have it. Every practical has a slot for your own protein, and that is where most people get their best result.

During the workshop

I cannot get ChimeraX installed. Email andres.posbeyikian@tsl.ac.uk before the workshop with your operating system and the error you are seeing. If it is still not working on the day, a facilitator will sort it out with you.

I have fallen behind. Take the pre-computed files from data/ for that session and rejoin. Do not try to catch up in silence. Flag a facilitator.

Can I record the sessions? Ask the speaker first.

Will slides be shared? Yes, in slides/, within two weeks of the workshop.

Where are the instructions for the practicals? On the Practicals pages of this site, one page per session. We update them as the sessions run and after the workshop, so they stay current.

Afterwards

Can I reuse these materials to teach my own group? Yes. Teaching materials are CC BY 4.0. A link back is appreciated.

How do I get help with my own project? Ask a facilitator during the workshop. Afterwards, email getgenome@tsl.ac.uk.
