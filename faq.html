FAQ

Is registration free? Registration is required but free, and it has now closed.

Do I need to bring a laptop? Yes. Computers are not provided at either venue.

What do I need to install beforehand? UCSF ChimeraX, and an AlphaFold Server account. See Before you arrive.

I cannot get ChimeraX installed. Email andres.posbeyikian@tsl.ac.uk before the workshop with your operating system and the error you are seeing. If it is still not working on the day, a facilitator will sort it out with you.

Do you cover travel? No. Please arrange your own transport to and from the venue.

Is lunch provided? Yes, lunch and refreshments are provided. Tell us about dietary requirements as early as you can.

What time should I arrive? Registration opens 08:30 on Day 1, and 09:00 on Day 2. We begin strictly on time.

Can I attend both workshops? They run the same programme, so there is no benefit to attending twice.

Will the materials be shared? Yes. Slides and worksheets are added to this site after the workshop.

Anything else? Email getgenome@tsl.ac.uk.
