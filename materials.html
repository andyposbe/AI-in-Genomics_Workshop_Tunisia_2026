Where everything lives

Each session has two decks: one that introduces the topic, and one for the exercises. The topic decks are on Resources. The exercise decks, worksheets and data are on the four Practicals pages.

For trainers and facilitators

Every practical has a folder of the same name in three places, so Practical 1 material always goes in something called practical-01_predict.

  • A deck that introduces a topic goes in slides/talks/.
  • A deck for the exercises goes in slides/practical-01_predict/ and so on.
  • Worksheets in the matching folder under worksheets/, data in the matching folder under data/.
  • PDF please, and no spaces in file names.
  • Do not upload someone else's talk without asking them first.
