The practical pages are the live version of the material. We edit them during the sessions and correct them afterwards, so refresh the page if you are following along on a laptop.

Slide decks are added within two weeks of each workshop.

For trainers and facilitators

To add your own slides or a worksheet, follow CONTRIBUTING.md in the repository. It is written for people who have never used GitHub: sign in, open the right folder, drag the file in, click commit. Nothing else.

In short:

  • Day 1 talks go in slides/day1/, Day 2 talks in slides/day2/, flash talks in slides/flash/.
  • Worksheets go in worksheets/practical-01_predict/ and the matching folders for the other three sessions.
  • Name files Topic_Surname.pdf for slides and P1-exercises.pdf for worksheets, with no spaces.
  • Export to PDF. Add the .pptx next to it only if the deck needs to stay editable.
  • Never upload someone else's flash talk without asking them first.

To fix something on a practical page, open that page and click Suggest a change to this page at the foot of it.
