Tools

Tool What it does Link
AlphaFold Server Structure and complex prediction, Practicals 1 and 2 https://alphafoldserver.com/
UCSF ChimeraX Molecular visualisation, used throughout https://www.cgl.ucsf.edu/chimerax/download.html
Protenix Open structure prediction, and PXDesign for binder generation in Practical 4 https://protenix-server.com/

InterProScan Protein domain and family annotation https://www.ebi.ac.uk/interpro/search/sequence/
TM-align Pairwise structural alignment, with a TM-score https://zhanggroup.org/TM-align/
Foldseek Structure search against whole databases https://search.foldseek.com/search
FoldMason Multiple structure alignment and tree https://search.foldseek.com/foldmason
ProteinMPNN Inverse folding: designs a sequence that folds into a given structure Colab notebook
