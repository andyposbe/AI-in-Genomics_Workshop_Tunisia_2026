# practical-01_predict

Sequences for the Predict session: the rice immune receptor Pikp-1 and three effector proteins secreted by *Pyricularia oryzae*, the fungus causing rice blast disease.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `pikp1_186-486.fasta` | Pikp-1, residues 186 to 486 |
| `pikp_hma.fasta` | The HMA domain on its own |
| `pathogen_proteins.fasta` | Three pathogen proteins: AVR-PikD, AVR-Pib, AVR-Pii |

Related experimental structure: PDB 5A6P, the Pikp-HMA homodimer (Maqbool *et al.* 2015, PMID 26304198).
