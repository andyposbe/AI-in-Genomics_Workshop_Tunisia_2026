# practical-01_predict

Sequences for the Predict session. The system is the rice immune receptor Pikp-1
and three effector proteins secreted by *Pyricularia oryzae*, the fungus causing
rice blast disease.

| File | What it is | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| `pikp1_186-486.fasta` | Pikp-1, residues 186–486 (integrated HMA domain and NB-ARC domain) | Exercise 1, Exercise 3 |
| `pikp_hma.fasta` | The HMA domain alone | Exercise 2 (n = 1 to 4) |
| `pathogen_proteins.fasta` | Pathogen_protein_1 (AVR-PikD), 2 (AVR-Pib), 3 (AVR-Pii) | Exercise 3 |

The effector identities are given here for facilitators. Participants work with
them as Pathogen_protein_1, 2 and 3 and form a hypothesis from the confidence
metrics alone.

Related experimental structure: PDB 5A6P, the Pikp-HMA homodimer
(Maqbool *et al.* 2015, PMID 26304198).
