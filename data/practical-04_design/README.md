# practical-04_design

Input files for the Design session. The worksheet for this session is
[`worksheets/Day2_Practicals-3-4_Worksheet.pdf`](../../worksheets/Day2_Practicals-3-4_Worksheet.pdf).

Two secreted effectors of *Fusarium oxysporum* f. sp. *albedinis*, the fungus
behind dieback of date palm. Exercise 6 takes one of them as the target and
designs binders against it on the PXDesign server. Exercise 5 needs no files.

## targets

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `FoaToxA.cif` | Predicted structure of the effector FoaToxA |
| `FoaKP6.cif` | Predicted structure of the effector FoaKP6 |

Both structures were predicted with AlphaFold Server, and both are usable in
PXDesign as they are. You may also bring a target of your own.
