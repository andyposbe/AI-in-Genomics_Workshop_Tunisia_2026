# Code of conduct

This workshop brings together researchers at very different career stages,
from different institutions and countries, working in different languages.
That mix is the point. It only works if everyone is treated as a colleague.

## What we expect

- Assume competence. Someone who has not used a terminal before is not a
  beginner at science.
- Ask questions freely, at any point. If you are confused, others are too.
- Answer questions without condescension. "That's a basic question" is never
  a useful reply.
- Respect that people are working in a second or third language. Speak
  clearly, avoid idiom, and be patient.
- Credit people's work properly, in the room and afterwards.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, demeaning comments, or discrimination of any kind — including on
the basis of nationality, gender, religion, disability, career stage or
institution. Sustained disruption of sessions. Photographing or sharing
someone's unpublished data without their permission.

## Reporting

Speak to any facilitator or trainer, or email <getgenome@tsl.ac.uk>. Reports
are handled confidentially. Organisers may ask someone to stop, or to leave.
