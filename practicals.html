Practical sessions

Four hands-on sessions across the two days. Each page holds the materials for that session, added during and after the workshop.

Session When
P1 Predict · Submit your sequence data to predict models, interpret and compare scores across complexes. Day 1, 11:20
P2 Interpret · Introduction to molecular visualisation to interpret protein models and design key experiments. Day 1, 15:15
P3 Compare · Align structures to reveal hidden relationships beyond sequence similarity. Day 2, 09:30
P4 Design · Design de novo structures and binders against a target to create novel solutions. Day 2, 13:30
